Native Android client for recording clock-in, clock-out, and break events.

Punches are saved on the device first and remain available offline. When a
configured API is reachable again, the app uploads the pending queue and
reconciles records using a stable identifier.

The monthly summary shows total worked time, extra-hour balance, and a financial
estimate based on the configured salary, workweek, and overtime rate.

The ESP32-C3 BH-Ponto accessory can be paired over Bluetooth Low Energy. A
short press records a punch on the phone, prevents duplicates, and refreshes
the list immediately. Through BLE2, the OLED receives today's hours, the
monthly extra-hour total, and the equivalent value.

Automatic punches are classified by time: the first is clock-in; the following
punches alternate break start and break end; only even-length sequences end
with clock-out, with no fixed punch limit.
